# Trip detection

The plugin emits evidence; Wake Logger remains authoritative for provisional and final trip boundaries.

The state machine is `STOPPED → START_CANDIDATE → MOVING → STOP_CANDIDATE → STOPPED`. A start candidate begins when SOG exceeds 1.5 kn or the vessel moves more than 200 m from its stationary cluster. Two minutes of credible movement confirms it and backdates the effective time. A stop candidate begins below 0.5 kn; remaining within 75 m for 15 minutes confirms it and backdates the effective end.

The stationary origin uses a slowly adjusted centroid only while points remain inside the 75 m berth cluster. This absorbs normal GPS drift without dragging the origin along a slow marina departure. Engine data is neither subscribed nor required, so engine-off sailing remains valid. An internet outage does not end a tracking session. A gap of more than 30 minutes between collected positions marks the previous recording interrupted, so a power-off cannot silently join separate outings. Evidence includes effective time, speed, distance and dwell duration. State is persisted across Signal K and vessel restarts.

A stable recording ID is assigned at the first departure candidate and included in every subsequent sample, including the 15-minute stop-confirmation period. Cancelled departure candidates close as `cancelled`. A confirmed stop carries a `complete` manifest with inclusive first/last device sequence numbers; its expected count is `lastSequence - firstSequence + 1`. Effective trip end precedes the final confirmation sample.

The optional wire field `recording` contains `id`, `startedAt` (Unix milliseconds), `firstSequence`, `state`, and for closed recordings `endedAt` and `lastSequence`. Status messages repeat manifests in `recordings`, rotating pages of up to 20 closed manifests plus the active recording. Closed metadata is retained until an application ACK includes an exact `{id, lastSequence, state}` match in `recordingAcks`; a sample sequence acknowledgement alone does not delete it. This manifest acknowledgement confirms that cloud metadata is durable, independently of whether the recording’s samples are all uploaded. Wake Logger must count unique usable receipts for both recording ID and sequence, report rejected or missing samples separately, and process only a closed complete recording once all expected samples have arrived. An interrupted recording must remain visibly incomplete.

Per-device recording checkpoints are fsynced before each durable outbox append. A power loss between checkpoint and append can declare a missing final sample; this deliberately prevents false completion. Closed manifests survive acknowledgements and restarts, allowing the cloud to reconcile delayed history independently of live arrival order.
