# Lessons

- For moving chart overlays, verify the displayed ray origins against the rendered arrow tip during animation and map pans. Sharing a geographic fix alone does not guarantee stable pixel attachment. Treat the boat and its vessel guide rays as one visual assembly.

- Nigel wants concise sailing instruments: use standard abbreviations and measured values. Do not add routine explanatory headings, readiness/authority paragraphs or connection summaries without a request. Keep changing values on fixed, non-wrapping rows.

- Removing requested UI text means removing its visible presentation, not relocating it into an overlay or banner. Do not introduce success notifications such as “Navigation updated” without a request. Verify tabs remain clickable after actual navigation actions.
