# Wake Logger for Signal K 0.2.0-beta.15

## Prepare sails for the whole course

The Race tab now shows the recommended sail plan and estimated apparent wind angle (AWA) and speed (AWS) for every remaining leg. The current sail plan sits beside the live navigation instruments, without a duplicate current-leg card. The leg after the next mark also shows true wind direction (TWD) and speed (TWS). Wind arrows show direction; apparent angles show port or starboard.

Planning uses the latest fresh vessel wind at startup and switches to a rolling five-minute average once five minutes of observations are available. Every remaining leg projects the selected wind onto its own course and estimated boat speed. Forecast wind is used when fresh usable vessel wind is unavailable. Completed legs keep their history.

Live AWA/AWS displays ease changes over 15 seconds, suppressing changes smaller than 1 degree or 0.2 knots. A different preferred sail must persist for 30 seconds before the displayed match changes. Stronger reefing recommendations apply immediately. Sail-range warning text has been removed from the leg cards. Fallback boat-speed estimates now vary continuously across wind-angle and wind-strength boundaries.

## Clearer onboard instruments

The Instruments tab now includes signed roll in degrees from the vessel attitude sensor. Missing or stale readings remain unavailable. Desktop panel tabs fit without clipping, including Charts.

## Upgrade and use

Update Wake Logger in the Signal K App Store, restart Signal K if prompted, and reload the onboard app. Keep existing pairing credentials. With the matching Wake Logger website update, select a Race Plan and turn Live tracking on before departure to synchronise its Race Pack. A previously synchronised, applicable pack supports onboard guidance without internet. Automatic tracking also provides an onboard preview while Wake Logger remains authoritative.

The website companion update provides live current/following-leg sail guidance and estimated AWA/AWS, together with roll in its vessel instruments. Website roll requires a fresh measured attitude in the device telemetry.

The upper live wind instruments and the leg planning wind use different processing and may differ; the planning wind becomes a five-minute average. Roll displays the sensor measurement without adding a calibration offset. This is a beta release; verify operation aboard before relying on it.
