POST /tracking/finish requires expectedRecordingId. Finish closes only that active recording, with complete state, final actually appended sequence and maximum committed capture timestamp. It writes no navigation point, native route or navigation control. Status-only terminal manifests use the existing wire format. New GPS movement can open a new recording; unrelated instrument updates cannot restart from cached GPS. Finish retries are harmless, including after restart and exact cloud acknowledgement. Receipt ACK is not confirmation. Additive recordingStatuses identify full accepted coverage and committed core processing; pending manifests continue MQTT status replay every normal interval on the same connection until exact ready, which remains durable after restart. Optional weather enrichment remains separately labelled.

Prepared but uncommitted tails refuse Finish rather than silently shortening declared recording coverage. Legacy checkpoints without committed bounds can recover from a retained actual outbox tail. If that tail has already been reclaimed and no committed bounds exist, Finish refuses to invent bounds; fresh navigation or the unchanged automatic stop is needed. Cloud preparation/readiness is separate from saving onboard and from telemetry queue-empty state.
