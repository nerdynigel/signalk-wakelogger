# Wake Logger for Signal K 0.2.0-beta.11

17 October 2026.

## Live tracking asks before it changes

Turning Live tracking on or off in the onboard app now asks you to confirm the change and names what will happen. The new state is only shown once it has been saved onboard, and if the setting changed somewhere else the app re-reads it rather than overwriting it. The Wake Logger plugin settings page can no longer switch live transmission on by itself.

**After updating, open the onboard Wake Logger app and confirm Live tracking ON to resume uploading.** Until then the plugin keeps recording locally and sends nothing. Pairing, settings and recorded trips are retained.

## ETA, VMG and wind you can trust

The Race tab's ETA and VMG now use your net progress along the current leg — the leg-average speed you are actually making good — rather than the native time-to-go or a single speed sample, and the value is labelled "Leg-average VMG (ground)". During the first minute of a leg, a matching Wake Logger leg plan may show a clearly labelled provisional estimate ("kn (plan)").

If the position, speed or course data needed for the estimate is missing, stale or out of sync, the ETA is withheld and the reason is shown instead; estimates outside normal sailing speeds or more than a day away are not displayed.

True wind angle is derived relative to the bow — never the boat's course — and separately for each velocity reference, so a ground-based result cannot stand in for water wind. Wind VMG is computed and labelled per reference (ground or water), and values that would mix references, or that are stale, are shown as unavailable.

## A clearer map, progress and finish

Course progress now shows the distance sailed along the active leg, so it advances smoothly between waypoints. Finish trip is always a local save: cloud connection, upload queue and race readiness never block it, and a failed attempt explains the exact cause with a safe retry path. The heading and tack/gybe guide label now names the current tack and stays visible, and the map frames the boat even before a course or track exists. Guides take no tide or leeway into account.

## Upgrade

Update Wake Logger from the Signal K App Store and restart Signal K if prompted. Your settings, pairing and recorded trips are retained. Then open the onboard app and confirm Live tracking ON if you want new trips uploaded.

This remains a beta release. Offline forecast and chart readiness must still be checked before relying on prepared offline race guidance.
