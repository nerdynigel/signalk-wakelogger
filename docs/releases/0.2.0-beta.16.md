# Wake Logger for Signal K 0.2.0-beta.16

- The boat marker moves smoothly between position updates.
- Centre on vessel follows the boat gently. Panning or zooming keeps you in control of the map.
- Heading and tack lines stay attached to the tip of the boat arrow.
- Tack and gybe estimates stay visible through brief helm and wave movements. They switch sides after the boat has stayed across the wind for ten seconds.
- BTW and DTW show the bearing and distance to the current waypoint in Instruments and Race.
- Instrument labels use sailing abbreviations such as AWA, AWS, TWS, TWD, SOG and HDG.
- Extra instrument headings, status paragraphs and navigation-success banners have been removed.
- Changing instrument values stay on the same rows instead of moving the layout.

Update the plugin in the Signal K App Store, restart Signal K if prompted, and reload the onboard app. Your pairing, settings and recorded trips are kept.

The matching Wake Logger website update also adds smoother live tracking, keeps stale instrument values visible in a subdued style, and shows existing navigation symbols instead of duplicate waypoint markers. These website changes require the updated Wake Logger website and server.
