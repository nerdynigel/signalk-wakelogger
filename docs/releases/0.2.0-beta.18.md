# Wake Logger for Signal K 0.2.0-beta.18

Faint animated downwind arrows now cover **Map** and **Map + data** on desktop and phone. Their size, weight and movement reflect true-wind strength, with gradual transitions between updates. Calm wind is labelled; old wind readings stop moving and then disappear. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

Choose **Change next mark** in Race to open a mark picker, or tap a course mark on the map to preview its distance and bearing. Confirm **Set point** or **Set as next mark** to change the target. **Advance point** moves to the mark after the current target. Opening the picker or highlighting a mark leaves navigation unchanged. The existing Course controls remain available.

AWA and TWA values are larger, while wind speed remains the main reading. Phones show a larger TWD beside true-wind speed. The true-wind heading is simpler and the cloud icon is centred. Upload status shows acknowledged-sample progress and an estimated time remaining when enough information is available.

Saved trips can begin uploading while optional course synchronisation is unavailable. Upload recovery preserves the age of retried data, and larger telemetry profiles use compatible batches. Unreadable route responses remain visible as errors and existing navigation stays protected.

Update Wake Logger in the Signal K App Store, restart Signal K if prompted, and reload the onboard app. Pairing, settings and recorded trips are retained. Fresh sensor data is required for live readings and wind arrows. Prepare local charts and a matching Race Pack before relying on offline chart and planning coverage.

This remains a beta release. During beta testing, npm's `latest` and `beta` aliases point to the same vetted prerelease for Signal K App Store discovery.
