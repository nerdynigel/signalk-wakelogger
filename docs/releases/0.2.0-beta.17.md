# Wake Logger for Signal K 0.2.0-beta.17

Choose **Map + data**, **Map** or **Instruments** from the onboard header. Map + data keeps the Instruments, Race, Course and Charts panels beside the chart; Map gives the chart the available width. Your choice stays in this browser tab across reloads.

The Instruments display has a heading-up compass with smooth dial and pointer movement between sensor updates. The numeric readings retain their normal refresh cadence. Compass and inner wind-angle labels share a larger font, the neutral 180° label is white, the magenta bearing line meets its pointer, and the heading sits above the dial without a box. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

On phones, the instrument dial fills the content width, SOG/STW cards are shorter and true-wind details use separate rows. Next-mark distance, time-to-go and arrival values are smaller; ETA uses 24-hour time. Longer time-to-go values identify hours and minutes. PT and STB follow angle values in smaller text.

**Next leg** previews planned wind and sail guidance without advancing the active course. Previous / Next controls have their own space, with the forecast update timestamp below. Return with **Previous** to see the current mark again.

Tap a connection indicator on desktop, or the cloud/vessel icon on a phone, for status and the **Live tracking** switch. A green vessel means the plugin is recording locally with live tracking off. **Finish trip** remains available in the header or phone recording bar.

Update Wake Logger in the Signal K App Store, restart Signal K if prompted, and reload the onboard app. Pairing, settings and recorded trips are retained. Live readings still require fresh sensor data; local charts and prepared Race Packs remain separate requirements for offline chart and planning coverage.
